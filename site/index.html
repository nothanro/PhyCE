Physics on your TI-84 Plus CE.

Fill in what you know. Get the answer with its units, and every step to get there, the way a worked example in your textbook does.

Free and open source. For the TI-84 Plus CE and TI-83 Premium CE.

What's new

Phy CE 1.0: FlowCE's look and its steps' page, made for physics. Seventeen kinds of problems in rotation, torque, equilibrium, moment of inertia, energy and angular momentum, each a form you fill in; answers with their units; ω, α, τ, φ and Δθ written as textbooks write them; the formula reference by topic; unit conversions for rpm, revolutions and degrees. See what's newHide
  • Motion: angular kinematics (three of Δθ, ω₀, ω, α and t give the other two, by the equation without the one you don't need), a point on a spinning object (vt, at, ac), period and frequency.
  • Torque: the torque of a force, with its angle to r or from the perpendicular; net torque, counterclockwise positive; the center of mass; a beam on two supports; the angle where a box tips.
  • Inertia: point masses, the parallel axis theorem, τ = Iα, a hanging mass on a heavy pulley, the Atwood machine with a heavy pulley.
  • Energy: rotational kinetic energy and the work of a torque, rolling down a ramp (a sphere, a disk, a hoop or any I = kMR²), a shape change (I₁ω₁ = I₂ω₂), two disks that grab.
  • Units: type a value with its unit (1200_rpm, 3_rev, 30_cm, 40_deg) and Phy CE converts it; every answer carries its unit. more → Unit conversions shows a conversion step by step.

Install

You need a TI-84 Plus CE, its USB cable, and Chrome or Edge on a computer. The page reads your OS and chooses the installer: v2.1 through 5.8.4, v3 on 5.8.5. Installing erases the calculator's apps and archived files.

This browser can't reach a calculator. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or TI Connect CE.
Windows can't open the calculator. First close TI Connect CE (while it runs it keeps the calculator, even hidden in the taskbar's corner) and any other tab of this page, unplug the calculator, plug it back in and press Connect calculator again. Still the same? Install TI Connect CE 6.1 or newer (its driver lets browsers reach the calculator), close it, then reconnect here.
  1. 1

    Connect

    Plug in the calculator and turn it on. Close TI Connect CE if it's open.

  2. 2

    Install

    Sends Phy CE.

  3. 3

    Finish on the calculator

    When it shows INST, press enter once. It installs and restarts by itself ("RAM Cleared"). Then apps → PhyCE.

Install with TI Connect CE: OS 5.3–5.8.4
  1. Clear the archive: 2nd + (MEM) → 7 → ▶ ARCHIVE → 3 → 2.
  2. Download PhyCE.b84 and send it with TI Connect CE.
  3. prgm → enter → enter opens arTIfiCE. enter starts INST, enter again installs.
  4. At "Success! Will now reset", press enter. Then clear, apps → PhyCE.
“Unknown OS version”, or updating the calculator

If INST shows “Unknown OS version”, reload this page and try its updated installer. If it still fails, report your model, OS and boot-code versions. Newer boot code still needs verification; upgrading the OS is not a confirmed fix. If Phy CE already works, keep your OS.

If you choose to update, TI currently offers OS 5.8.5. TI says you cannot downgrade afterward.

  1. Back up the calculator's files to your computer with TI Connect CE.
  2. Use TI's update page and choose your exact calculator model.
  3. Keep the calculator connected and powered. In TI Connect CE, choose Actions → Send OS/Bundles to Calculators…, select the downloaded OS file, then Send. Wait until the transfer and restart finish. TI's illustrated instructions.
  4. Close TI Connect CE. Return here and connect again: this page detects 5.8.5 and selects arTIfiCE v3.
Install on OS 5.8.5 with TI Connect CE

Back up your files first. Phy CE replaces the other apps. Keep your current OS; upgrading isn't needed to use Phy CE.

  1. Clear the archive before starting: 2nd + → 7 → ▶ ARCHIVE → 3 → 2.
  2. Follow the official arTIfiCE v3 instructions for OS 5.8.5: install Inequalz, reset RAM, send arTIfiCE.8xp to RAM, open Inequalz's Functions screen, then send INEQUVAR.8xv. After the restart, press on if the screen stays black.
  3. apps → AsmHook2. Dismiss “Installation successful!” to return to the home screen.
  4. Download Phy CE. Send the bundle with TI Connect CE. Keep INST in RAM and AppIns files in Archive. Keep AsmHook2 until you run INST; do not reset or erase again between these steps.
  5. prgm → select INST by name → enter → enter. Ignore the bundle's program A; it is for older OS versions. Press enter at the install prompt, then wait for the countdown to finish.
  6. At “Success! Will now reset”, press enter. After “RAM Cleared”, clear, then apps → PhyCE.

FlowCE's installer, verified in CEmu on OS 5.8.5.0074 with boot code 5.6.1.0006. Calculators with newer boot code still need verification. If INST says “Unknown OS version”, report your model, OS and boot-code versions.

Trouble?
  • Connect fails on Windows: update TI Connect CE to 6.1 or newer, then unplug and replug the calculator.
  • Connect fails on a Mac or Chromebook: close TI Connect CE, then unplug and replug.
  • Linux: add a udev rule such as SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0451", ATTRS{idProduct}=="e008", TAG+="uaccess" in /etc/udev/rules.d/69-ti-calc.rules, then replug.
  • Still not enough memory after the erase: TI's language apps survive it. The page offers to delete them (Delete the language apps and continue); by hand: 2nd + → 2 → Apps, del on each.
  • "RAM Cleared" but this page still waits: it is installed. Press apps → PhyCE.
  • Frozen at "Success! Will now reset": press the reset button on the back (a pen tip). Phy CE stays installed.
  • The calculator acts strangely: clear the RAM: 2nd + → 7 → 1 → 2. Phy CE stays installed.
  • OS 5.8.5: the page selects v3. Follow its calculator prompts, or use TI Connect CE. OS 5.8.4.0058 uses v2.1.
  • INST says “Unknown OS version”: see OS help and update instructions. Updating is not a confirmed fix for newer boot code.

Something else, or a wrong answer? Report it on GitHub.
